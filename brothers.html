01 / WHO WE ARE

Brothers first.
Artists in our own right.

We share a love of performing, but bring different personalities to the work. Acting gives us room to step into someone else’s life. Music gives us a place to bring our own. We’re pursuing opportunities individually and together, with a natural connection that comes from being brothers.

Daniel Turanov

Actor · Singer · Musician

Daniel is a St. Louis–based actor and performer with leading and supporting film credits. Alongside his screen work, he sings, plays guitar, and writes and performs with David.

His background spans acting for film, improvisation, and martial arts, with fluent Russian and a strong interest in character-driven work.

David Turanov

Actor · Singer · Dancer

David is an actor, singer, and dancer, and Daniel’s younger brother. He brings his own personality and love of performance to their work together, on camera and on stage.

As one half of Discromatic, David sings and dances alongside Daniel. He is pursuing acting and performance opportunities both individually and with his brother.
